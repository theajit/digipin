by default.

PinID Lite stores your claimed alias, an encrypted location and hashed invitation, recovery and session credentials. Your location is private unless you explicitly enable public sharing. Public lookup reveals only your handle, DIGIPIN and precise coordinates; no private notes are returned.

The necessary owner cookie lasts one hour, is HttpOnly and SameSite Strict, and is Secure in production. There are no analytics, third-party maps, marketing cookies or browser localStorage credentials. Browser geolocation runs only when you request it.

Save your recovery credential in a password manager. It is displayed once during enrollment. Anyone holding it can access your account. If lost, it cannot be retrieved; this beta has no email recovery or government identity verification. Administrator invitations are bearer credentials delivered privately, not proof of recipient identity.

Export and deletion are available in your dashboard. Location deletion removes the live encrypted record; account deletion revokes sessions and recovery access and preserves the alias as a tombstone to prevent impersonation. Database journals and encrypted backups may retain deleted records until maintenance/retention expiry. Operators must publish their contact and actual backup/audit retention policy before inviting users.

DIGIPIN represents a geographic grid cell. It does not establish residence or address ownership. This independent service uses India Post's open-source DIGIPIN implementation and is not officially affiliated with India Post.

Interest requests and public sharing

Invitation requests store your name, phone, email and desired handle encrypted for platform administrator review. Requests do not reserve handles. Ask the operator to delete your request; administrators can delete contact records. Public sharing is off by default. Enabling it exposes only your handle, DIGIPIN and precise coordinates to anyone. Disable it to prevent future retrieval; previously copied information cannot be recalled. Changing or deleting a location turns public sharing off.

Invitations and email requests

If you invite someone, their name, email and phone are stored encrypted for invitation administration. One invitation per account is available. Sending uses Resend: the recipient receives their invitation code, while the PinID team at invite@pincode.cafe and ajit@pincode.cafe receives contact details and activity notifications. Additional-handle requests also send your provided contact details and requested username to that team through Resend. No private location, address notes or sign-in keys are included in these emails.

Your account export includes requests linked to your account. Account deletion removes those saved request contacts; the invitation quota and redacted invitation history remain. Email providers and recipients may retain copies that PinID cannot delete. Inbox delivery is not guaranteed by API acceptance. Additional handles cost ₹199/year, but this release only accepts requests and does not take payments or activate extra handles.
